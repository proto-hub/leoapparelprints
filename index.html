Industrial Machine Lineup

High-Speed Roll

High-Speed Sublimation Printer

Continuous roll-to-roll high-speed dye-sublimation printer supporting up to 72-inch width. Produces vivid neon and true black shades on polyester fabrics.

  • Daily Throughput: 15,000 Meters / day
  • Maximum Width: 72 Inch (180 cm)
  • Application: Sportswear, Flags, Home Textiles
Direct Textile

Continuous Digital Belt Printer

Advanced adhesive belt conveyer printer with precision tension control for high-resolution pigment printing on stretchable and delicate natural fibers.

  • Daily Throughput: 10,000 Meters / day
  • Suitable Fabrics: 100% Cotton, Rayon, Modal, Linen
  • Inks: Eco-Friendly GOTS Pigment Inks
Hybrid DTG

Carousel Hybrid DTG Station

Combines automatic screen printing underbase with high-definition digital inkjet heads. Provides vibrant chest prints on dark and black cotton garments.

  • Daily Throughput: 15,000 Pieces / day
  • Maximum Print Size: 60 X 80 cm (Bit Form)
  • Special Effects: High Density, Foil, Discharge Base
Heavy Duty

Industrial AOP Pigment System

Heavy-duty multi-head industrial textile engine built for continuous uninterrupted production runs. Ideal for large apparel export houses and domestic brands.

  • Capacity: 10,000 Meters / day
  • Repeat Size: Seamless Infinite Repeat
  • Color Channels: 8-Color High Gamut CMYK+OBG
Alfa DTF System

Alfa Industrial DTF System

High-volume Direct-to-Film roll printer equipped with automatic hot-melt powder application, shaking, and infrared curing tunnel for crack-free transfers.

  • Daily Throughput: 25,000 Pieces / day
  • Print Width: Up to 48 Inch Width
  • Peel Type: Hot & Cold Instant Peel
